# GAWAZ INN 2.0 — Core User Journeys

## Journey 1: 10-Year Early Start Planner ("Thinking About Marriage")
1. User registers and selects stage **"Thinking About Marriage"** / **"Preparing Early"**.
2. Chooses **10-year horizon** inside **GAWAZ INN EARLY START** and opens **My Future Fund**.
3. Uses the **Total Marriage Budget** & **Savings What-If Calculator** (`1,000 EGP/mo` vs `3,000 EGP/mo`, annual inflation adjustments).
4. Creates separate private goals (`Apartment Purchase`, `Finishing`, `Appliances`, `Wedding`, `Emergency Reserve`).
5. Logs monthly savings contributions → earns private milestone streak badges → reads **Early Start Academy** financial guides.
6. Years later, switches lifecycle stage to **Preparing for Engagement** → all savings history and goals seamlessly transition into the **Engagement Planner** & **Budget Manager**.

## Journey 2: Apartment Finishing & Groom/Bride Preparation
1. Engaged couple connects accounts via **Shared Wedding Workspace** invitation (granular permissions).
2. Reads **Apartment Finishing Encyclopedia** (22 stages, Ceramic vs Porcelain material comparisons) → opens **Finishing Calculator** (`140 m²`, Cairo, Medium/Luxury package) → generates itemized breakdown & saves target to **Budget Manager**.
3. Uses **Groom's Preparation** & **Bride's Trousseau** checklists to detect missing items, compares 3 washing machines side-by-side (`ProductCompare`), builds multi-merchant shopping lists, and requests a multi-vendor **RFQ** for turnkey finishing.

## Journey 3: Wedding Planning & Big Day Execution
1. Couple sets expected wedding date → **Wedding Journey Planner** generates customized 12-month-to-post-wedding checklist with Bride/Groom/Shared task assignments.
2. Filters verified venues & photographers in Cairo/Giza → books venue visit **Appointment** → receives vendor confirmation → compares received quotes → accepts offer → **Budget Manager** automatically records deposit without duplicate entries.
3. Creates **Digital Wedding Invitation**, manages **Guest RSVP**, arranges **Seating Tables**, and publishes **Gift Registry**.

## Journey 4: Vendor Onboarding, Verification & Lead Management
1. Business registers a **Vendor** account and uploads verification documents.
2. Admin reviews credentials in **Admin OS** and grants `Verified` badge (independent of paid subscription tier).
3. Vendor configures services, galleries, and time slots in **Vendor Portal**.
4. Vendor receives matched **RFQ**, submits itemized quote, converts customer Q&A into **Vendor CRM** stages (`New Lead → Quotation Sent → Negotiation → Accepted → Completed`).

## Journey 5: Post-Wedding Married Life & Home Care
1. After wedding date, user transitions stage to **Newly Married**.
2. Transfers purchased appliances & finishing details into **Digital Home File** and **Home Inventory** (serial numbers, paint codes, invoices).
3. Sets **Warranty Tracker** expiration dates and **Maintenance Reminders** (AC service every 6 months), and discovers trusted technicians in the **Home Care Directory**.

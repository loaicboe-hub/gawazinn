# GAWAZ INN 2.0 — Product Vision

## 1. Mission Statement
GAWAZ INN does not simply help people organize a wedding. It helps people **prepare for their future, build their home, and manage their journey into married life**.

## 2. The 14-Stage Continuous Lifecycle
1. Thinking about marriage.
2. Preparing financially years before engagement (up to 10+ years ahead via **Early Start**).
3. Building savings and financial discipline (**My Future Fund**, Streaks, Academy).
4. Learning about marriage, real estate, and household responsibilities.
5. Preparing for engagement (**Engagement Planner**).
6. Planning the wedding (**Wedding Journey Planner**).
7. Finding and inspecting the apartment.
8. Finishing and furnishing the apartment (**Apartment Finishing Encyclopedia & Calculators**).
9. Preparing the groom's requirements (**Groom's Preparation Encyclopedia & Packages**).
10. Preparing the bride's requirements (**Bride's Trousseau Encyclopedia & Checklists**).
11. Organizing the wedding day (**Farahi Marketplace, Guest/Seating, Invitations, Registry**).
12. Moving into the new home (**Moving Planner**).
13. Managing household needs after marriage (**Newlyweds Guide, Household Budget, Home Inventory**).
14. Maintaining and improving the home (**Home Care Directory, Digital Home File, Warranty Tracker**).

## 3. Core Ecosystem Connecting Principle
Nothing in GAWAZ INN exists as an isolated page:
- Encyclopedia articles & material guides link directly to **Smart Calculators**.
- Calculators generate **Shopping Lists**, **Checklists**, and **Budget Categories**.
- Shopping Lists connect to structured **Products**, **Price Comparisons**, and **Verified Vendors**.
- Vendors respond to user **RFQs** and **Appointment Requests** via the **Vendor Portal & CRM**.
- Accepted quotes, bookings, and purchases update the user's **Wedding Budget Manager** and **My Future Fund** without duplicate transactions.
- Couples collaborate safely inside a **Shared Workspace** with explicit invitations and permissions.
- Every step preserves historical progress when transitioning across any of the **11 user lifecycle stages**.
